Not sure which jurisdiction you are in?

If you live in unincorporated Wake County, Wake County handles your permits. Raleigh and Cary run their own departments, as do Apex, Wake Forest, and other municipalities. Wake County's iMaps site tells you your planning jurisdiction: if “WC” is listed, you are in Wake County's jurisdiction.
